How Reasoning Evolves from Post-Training Data

Chess is a remarkably clean observatory for reasoning. We fine-tuned a 7B-parameter language model with supervised and reinforcement learning, outperformed strong open-source reasoning models in chess, then studied how different theoretically inspired datasets changed both performance and the shape of the model's reasoning.
