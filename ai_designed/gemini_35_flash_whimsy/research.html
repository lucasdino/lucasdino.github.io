Lucas Dionisopoulos (Lead Author) • UCSD PEARLS Lab

PCFG Search

A* Neural Guided Search on ARC-AGI

Lucas Dionisopoulos • Neurosymbolic Systems

Built a synthetic data generation pipeline to train a modified iBOT image embedding model from scratch. Integrated this network with a Probabilistic Context-Free Grammar (PCFG) to formulate a hybrid neurosymbolic search engine designed to solve tasks on the Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus (ARC).

Philosophize That: Classification of Personal Thoughts

Lucas Dionisopoulos • Philosophy & AI

Created a custom corpus of philosophical texts using various filtering techniques to isolate semantic signal from noisy academic datasets. Trained an NLP classifier using document-level embeddings to identify the underlying philosophical school of thought behind personal journal entries.
