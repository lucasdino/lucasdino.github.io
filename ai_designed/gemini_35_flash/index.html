The Journey / How I got here

UCSD PEARLS Lab & Hadrian

Los Angeles & San Diego · Graduate Researcher & Engineer

I work on reinforcement learning (RL) and post-training methods for Large Language Models. At UC San Diego's PEARLS Lab, I study how SFT and RL data shape qualitative reasoning behaviors, especially using sandboxed environments like chess. Our team published this work at ICML 2026 and presented an Oral talk at the NeurIPS FoRLM workshop.

Alongside research, I work on Special Projects at Hadrian, acting as a technical generalist and builder to automate precision defense manufacturing through advanced software and systems integration.

Self-Study, Airbnbs & Flight Decks

Global Sabbatical · Gap Year ML/CS Intensive

Realizing I wanted to shift from finance models to code, I designed a math and computer science intensive gap year. I studied linear algebra, multivariate calculus, and ML basics full-time.

To stretch my savings, I lived out of airport lounges, long-term Airbnbs, and pet sits across Europe, Asia, and North America. While traveling, I wrote baseline RL gyms, autonomous vehicle path planners, and custom neural search engines. Read about what I studied on my year off here.

FT Partners

San Francisco & Los Angeles · Investment Banking Analyst

Spent two intense years working on mergers and acquisitions (M&A) and capital-raising transactions in the fintech industry.

Investment banking taught me extreme diligence under pressure and how markets and incentives align. I even kept a detailed log of my honest working hours (which averaged 80+ hours/week). Over time, I recognized that I wanted to build systems rather than model them. Read my thoughts on why I pivoted.

Washington University in St. Louis

St. Louis, MO · Undergrad Finance & Ops

Studied finance and operations in undergrad, learning how economic systems operate. St. Louis is where I first got interested in the Midwest-to-tech pipelines.

During college, I wanted to do something tangible for the local community, so I founded a project called Clean Our Green, organizing volunteers to clean up and restore St. Louis city parks.
