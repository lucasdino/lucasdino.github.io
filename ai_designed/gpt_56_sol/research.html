How Reasoning Evolves from Post-Training Data: An Empirical Study Using Chess

We fine-tuned and reinforced a 7B-parameter language model on theoretically motivated chess datasets, then tracked what happened to its performance, hallucinations, stability, and reasoning quality.

The strongest policy was not the most faithful reasoner. Multi-move training produced comparable performance with more stable RL and reasoning that better matched the selected move.

Our released system surpassed leading open-source reasoning models in chess. The broader aim was not to solve chess, but to use it as a controlled laboratory for post-training.
