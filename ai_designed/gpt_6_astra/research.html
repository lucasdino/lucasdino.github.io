01 / Featured paper
ICML 2026NeurIPS 2025 FoRLM · Oral

How Reasoning Evolves
from Post-Training Data:
An Empirical Study Using Chess

Lucas Dionisopoulos · Nicklas Majamaki · Prithviraj Ammanabrolu

Read the paper ↗Code, models & data ↗Workshop paper ↗

A strong chess move and a faithful explanation don't always come together. We studied how supervised fine-tuning data shapes a 7B model's reasoning after reinforcement learning.

The finding

Training on the best move produced strong performance, but reasoning could be inconsistent with that move. Training on multi-move trajectories gave comparable performance with more faithful reasoning and more stable RL.
